You add evaluations of AI safety methods to OpenAudit. The repo is the root of your OpenAudit clone (call it R).
A method eval scores methods that inspect a model (SAEs, probes, lenses, monitors), not the models. Your evals
are listed at the end, after "EVALS:". Follow R/ADD_A_METHOD_EVAL.md, steps 1 to 8, for each one.
Do not do step 5 (the data of each item). If the source has data for each item, say so in your reply.

LIMITS
- Write only these files: R/data/seed/evals/<slug>.json, R/data/seed/eval-suites/<slug>.json (for a suite),
  R/data/scripts/fetch-<source>.mjs and R/data/extracted/methods/<source>.json. If a method or a variant is
  missing, stop and say so in your reply; do not edit R/data/seed/methods/.
- Do not commit, push or upload anything. Do not import to production, and do not delete rows.
- The import (--apply) and the method card build on the dev database are allowed. A card build costs a few cents.
- Never print secrets: filter postgres:// out of any output you show.

FACTS
- Every fact must come from the eval's paper, code, data or official page. Open each source. Do not trust
  the summary of a search result. Use arXiv /html/<id> for full papers and the arXiv export API for dates.
- Results come from a script that reads the source (result files, a paper table). Never type a number.
- Check for placeholders: values such as -1 or 0 that mean "not computed". Drop them in the fetch script.
- Write the limits of the eval in its "description" (models, data, age, who ran it, what it does not
  test).

STYLE
- ASD-STE100 Simplified Technical English: short sentences, active voice, common words.
- "summary": one sentence. "description": what it does, on which models, how it scores, its limits.

CARDS
- Build the method cards of each method that the eval tests, then read each draft. Test each claim against
  the input. If a claim comes from bad data, fix the data and build again. Publish only drafts in which
  every claim is in the input.

REPLY
For each eval: its slug, its suite (if any), the methods and variants with results, the number of
artifacts and results imported, the card cost, and anything that you could not confirm. Keep it short.
